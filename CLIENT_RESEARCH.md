# R1DE Fairfield: educational adaptation

Research date: October 23, 2026.

## Business and services
Community-based indoor cycling studio at 85 Mill Plain Road, Building B (The Sportsplex), Fairfield, Connecticut. Official class descriptions list ONE R1DE (45-minute rhythm cycling), HIIT 1T, S1XTY, Spin & Sculpt, and Sculpt. Memberships, class packages, and private rides are described on the pricing page. The adaptation links to the business for current schedules and prices rather than presenting a working booking system.

## Audience and identity
The official website welcomes all fitness levels, including riders who follow the coach and those who choose their own pace. The pricing page includes a student membership. The intended audience is local adults and students seeking coached, music-led group exercise and community; this audience interpretation follows those service descriptions. The five stated values are Care, Motivate, Trust, Challenge, and Laugh. The official logo uses pale aqua with a warm gold numeral 1. The concept applies that palette, bold condensed type, and the client's own studio photographs to the existing five-chapter interactive template.
